About

Independent, learner-first educational publishing.

Salford Press is an independent press focused on practical, well-designed educational titles for EMT and Nursing learners.

Mission

Our aim is to publish high-quality educational books that are clear, consistent, and useful for structured practice.

How we work

Editorial development

We focus on structure, clarity, and consistency so learners can move through material confidently.

Production

Layout and formatting are reviewed for usability, print-readability, and a clean learning flow.

Release discipline

We maintain edition control and incorporate corrections to keep materials consistent over time.

Partnership fit
